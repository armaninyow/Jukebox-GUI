# Jukebox GUI
<details>
  <summary></summary>
   
A lightweight mod that adds a custom management screen to the vanilla Jukebox block.
</details>

![Mod Icon](src/main/resources/assets/jukeboxgui/icon.png)

## Functionality
<details>
  <summary></summary>
   
Shift+Right-Click any Jukebox to open the GUI. Inside you'll find:

- A **disc slot** that accepts only music discs
- The **song title** (Artist – Title) displayed with animated rainbow text while playing, and in black while stopped
- A **progress bar** that fills as the track plays, with a unique colored fill texture per disc
- A **digital clock** showing elapsed time and total song duration (MM:SS)

The timer persists in the background while the GUI is closed and correctly syncs whether the disc was inserted via the GUI or via a vanilla right-click. On world reload, the timer resets to 00:00 until you manually reinsert a disc.

Regular Right-Click still works as vanilla — you can insert and eject discs without ever opening the GUI.
</details>

## Benefits
<details>
  <summary></summary>
   
- Always know how far through a song you are without counting seconds
- Manage discs through a clean dedicated interface instead of relying on right-click timing
- The animated song title makes it immediately clear when music is actively playing vs. stopped
- No configuration required — works out of the box on any world
</details>

## Installation
<details>
  <summary></summary>
   
1.  **Requirements**: Ensure you have Minecraft 1.21.10, Fabric Loader 0.18.4, and the Fabric API installed.
2.  **Download**: Get the latest `.jar` from [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/jukebox-gui) or [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/jukebox-gui).
3.  **Setup**: Drop the file into your `%appdata%/.minecraft/mods` folder.
</details>
